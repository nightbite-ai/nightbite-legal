# NightBite Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

NightBite ("NightBite", "we", "us", or "our") is provided by Loompoint Labs LLC. This Privacy Policy explains what information NightBite collects, how that information is used, when information is shared with service providers, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Information We Collect

NightBite may collect information that you provide when you create an account, set up your profile, use the app, or request food recommendations.

### Account Information

We collect your email address to create and manage your NightBite account and authenticate your access to the app.

### Profile Information

You may provide profile information such as:

- Nickname
- Birth year
- Height
- Weight
- Biological sex
- Diet goal
- Usual late-night eating time

This information is stored as part of your NightBite profile. Not all profile information is currently used to generate AI recommendations.

### Dietary Information

You may provide information about your dietary needs and preferences, including:

- Allergies and allergy-related notes
- Dietary restrictions
- Food preferences, including foods you like or dislike

### Daily Activity and Food Information

You may enter information about:

- Meals and food consumed
- Exercise or physical activity
- Grocery purchases
- Hunger level
- Available food preparation time
- Current food cravings or preferences

### Inventory Information

You may provide information about food you currently have available, including:

- Food name
- Quantity
- Unit
- Expiration date
- Notes

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through NightBite to:

- Create and maintain your account
- Save and display your profile and dietary preferences
- Maintain your food inventory and daily logs
- Generate personalized late-night food recommendations
- Apply allergy and dietary restriction safeguards
- Save recommendations that you explicitly indicate you ate
- Operate, maintain, secure, and improve NightBite

## 3. AI-Powered Recommendations and Google Gemini

NightBite uses the Google Gemini API to generate food recommendations.

When you request an AI recommendation, NightBite may send the following information to Google Gemini:

- Hunger level
- Available preparation time
- Current food craving or preference
- Meal log for the current NightBite day
- Exercise log for the current NightBite day
- Grocery log for the current NightBite day
- Available inventory items, including quantity, unit, expiration date, and notes
- Allergies, allergy severity, and related notes
- Dietary restrictions and related notes
- Food preferences

Free-text fields and notes may contain information that you choose to provide.

NightBite does not currently send your email address, nickname, birth year, height, weight, biological sex, or NightBite user ID to Google Gemini as part of the recommendation request.

Before NightBite sends information to Google Gemini for AI recommendation generation, NightBite will disclose this data sharing and request your permission. You can withdraw your permission at any time through the app's Profile screen.

NightBite uses a paid Gemini API service. Under Google's terms for Paid Services, Google does not use prompts or generated responses to improve its products. Google may retain prompts and responses for a limited period for abuse prevention, security, and applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Google's processing and retention of information are governed by its applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 4. Recommendation History

Generating a recommendation does not by itself save that recommendation to your NightBite recommendation history.

If you explicitly select "I Ate This," NightBite stores information associated with the accepted recommendation. This may include:

- The recommendation that was generated
- Hunger level
- Available preparation time
- Current preference or craving
- The recommendation input context used to generate that recommendation
- The selected recommendation
- Related timestamps and feedback, when applicable

The stored recommendation input context may include your daily logs, inventory information, allergies, dietary restrictions, and food preferences.

This information is stored so NightBite can maintain your meal and recommendation history.

## 5. Service Providers

NightBite relies on third-party service providers to operate the app.

### Supabase

NightBite uses Supabase for services including account authentication, database storage, server-side functions, and related backend infrastructure.

Information stored in your NightBite account may therefore be processed or stored using Supabase infrastructure.

### Google Gemini

NightBite uses the Google Gemini API to generate AI-powered food recommendations. The information described in Section 3 may be transmitted to Google for this purpose.

We use service providers only as necessary to provide and operate NightBite, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

## 6. Data Retention

NightBite retains account and app data while your account remains active and as reasonably necessary to provide the service.

Certain recommendation information is stored only when you explicitly indicate that you ate a recommendation.

When you delete your NightBite account using the in-app account deletion feature, NightBite deletes your authentication account and associated application data from its active application database.

Deletion from NightBite's application database does not necessarily result in immediate deletion of information retained in service-provider backups, security logs, or other systems governed by the providers' applicable retention policies.

## 7. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your NightBite account directly within the app.

When account deletion is completed, your NightBite authentication account and associated data in NightBite's active application database are deleted, subject to the retention limitations described in Section 6.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you have questions about account or data deletion, you may contact us using the email address below.

## 8. Your Choices

You control the information you choose to enter into NightBite.

You may:

- Edit your profile information
- Edit your dietary information
- Edit your inventory information
- Edit your daily logs
- Choose whether to indicate that you ate a recommendation
- Choose whether to request an AI-generated recommendation
- Choose whether to consent to sharing recommendation-related information with Google Gemini
- Withdraw your AI data-sharing consent through the app's Profile screen
- Delete your NightBite account

You may choose not to provide optional information. However, some information may be necessary for particular NightBite features to function properly.

If you withdraw your AI data-sharing consent, NightBite will stop generating new AI recommendations until you provide consent again. Withdrawing consent does not retroactively delete information previously processed by Google or recommendation history already saved in NightBite.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through NightBite.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Children's Privacy

NightBite is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take appropriate steps to delete that information.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as NightBite changes.

If we make changes, we may update the Effective Date shown at the top of this policy. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or by other appropriate means.
